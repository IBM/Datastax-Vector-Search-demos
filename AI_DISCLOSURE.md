# AI-assisted development disclosure

This collection includes AI-assisted code, documentation, tests and refactoring. Assistance in the current development workflow is established; a complete historical, per-line model provenance record is not available.

The owner identifies these model families as potentially involved: Anthropic Claude Opus 4 and later, Claude Sonnet 3.7 and later, and OpenAI GPT-4 and later. This is a conservative scope notice, not a claim that every listed model generated every file or that exact model versions have been verified. No model is named as a human contributor or copyright owner.

Every maintained Python source and project/dependency/workflow configuration file has a header and every tutorial has a visible opening disclosure. The manifest [reports/ai-provenance.json](reports/ai-provenance.json) identifies those files and hashes. The disclosure covers potential AI involvement in drafting, modification, review and tests; it does not replace third-party attribution or establish originality, authorship or redistribution rights. The unmodified DCO and third-party license texts retain their original notices.

Contributors remain responsible for understanding, testing and reviewing their contributions, verifying rights to submit under Apache-2.0, preserving third-party notices, and personally making any required DCO certification. Record the provider, model/version and affected files when known; say unknown when records do not establish them. Do not include private prompts or customer data as provenance evidence.
