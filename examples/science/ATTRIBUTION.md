# Attribution for the science tutorials

All data, circuit test cases, prose, and implementation in these tutorials were newly written for this collection. No customer or prospect material, private notebook outputs, course artwork, saved credentials, or remote grading payloads are included.

The tutorials `teleportation`, `remote-cnot`, and `noise-calibration` develop topics taught in [Qiskit Global Summer School 2025](https://github.com/qiskit-community/qgss-2025), whose repository is distributed under Apache License 2.0. The course license is reproduced in [LICENSE.qgss-2025.txt](LICENSE.qgss-2025.txt). These rewrites replace the original exercises, code, data, and presentation with independent local experiments; they are not unmodified copies or official course solutions.

QGSS Lab 2 credits Jorge Martínez de Lejarza and Alberto Maldonado as creators, and Marcel Pfaffhauser, Paul Nation, Junye Huang, and Sophy Shin as advisers.
